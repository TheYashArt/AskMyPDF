# YouTube Summarizer Workflow

This workflow explains how to generate summaries for YouTube videos using their transcripts.

## 1. Navigate to YouTube Summary
Launch the app and select the **YouTube Summary** page from the sidebar navigation.

## 2. Enter Video URL
- Paste the full URL of the YouTube video you want to summarize (e.g., `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`).
- The app will automatically attempt to load the video and extract the transcript/captions.

## 3. Generate Summary
- Once the transcript is loaded, click the **Summarize Video** button.
- The AI will process the transcript (splitting it into chunks if necessary) and provide a concise summary.

> [!NOTE]
> This feature requires the video to have available captions or transcripts (English or Hindi preferred).
